    FAQ

    Quick answers

    How long does a typical repair take?

    Most repairs are completed within 24–48 hours. Parts that need to be ordered, or deep motherboard work, can take longer. We'll tell you upfront.

    Will I get a quote before work begins?

    Yes. We diagnose first, quote the cost, and only proceed once you approve.

    Do you repair all brands?

    Yes, including Dell, HP, Lenovo, Asus, Acer, Apple and more.

    Can you recover data from a failed drive?

    We handle data recovery for HDDs and SSDs. Success depends on the type of failure, so switch the device off and bring it in as soon as possible.
